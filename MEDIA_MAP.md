## Display rules

1. Authority, environment, and composite images use `object-fit: contain`; edge presenters must never be cropped away.
2. Before/after assets use the same scene and framing.
3. Motion posters come from the exact clips they represent.
4. Contact sheets must remain legible at phone width; web-native layouts replace ultra-wide evidence strips.
5. A caption must state what the visitor should notice.
6. Generated or rejected evidence is labeled honestly. It is never presented as final artwork.
7. No visual may be chosen merely because it is attractive. It must prove the adjacent claim.
